Privacy Policy

Frostbound Sentinel · Last updated September 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how junhao hong handles information in Frostbound Sentinel.

Information stored on your device

The game stores gameplay progress, stage completion, hero materials, game settings, and reward status locally on your device so that the game can operate and restore your progress.

Advertising and rewarded ads

The game may use third-party advertising providers, including Chartboost, Yandex Mobile Ads, Vungle, and InMobi, to provide optional rewarded advertisements. These providers may process device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostic information according to their own privacy policies.

Where required, the game requests your permission before accessing the device advertising identifier for personalized advertising. You may decline permission or change that choice later in your device settings. Rewarded ads are optional and are not required to enter or play a stage.

How information is used

Local data is used to operate the game. Advertising-related information is used by advertising providers to deliver, measure, limit frequency of, and help prevent fraud in advertisements.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by the advertising providers described above when you interact with advertising features.

Children

Frostbound Sentinel is not directed to children under the age at which consent is required in their jurisdiction. If you believe a child has provided information through an advertising feature, please use the support channel listed on the App Store product page.

Changes

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the game or applicable requirements. The updated date at the top of this page will be revised when changes are made.
