An original fantasy defense adventure

Frostbound Sentinel

Defend the Rune Wall through a frozen frontier. Summon a team of heroes, merge matching ranks into unpredictable new allies, and stop ten escalating waves before the fortress falls.

Defend the frontier

  • Command Archer, Warrior, Mage, and Support heroes.
  • Summon, place, and merge matching heroes to create stronger ranks.
  • Prioritize bosses, manage coins, and protect the Rune Wall.
  • Battle through 50 replayable stages with 10 waves each.
  • Collect class sigils to upgrade heroes up to level 30.

Rewards and choices

Daily rewards grant upgrade materials and limited in-battle bonuses. Optional rewarded ads may offer an extra continuation or doubled sigil rewards after a clear. Watching an ad is never required to start a stage.
